TerraGrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · efb apps

Short version: TerraGrid does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we have no servers. Your location leaves your device only when you turn on team broadcasting, and the waypoints and routes you choose to share go only to your team, through your own iCloud.

1. Data we collect

None. TerraGrid has no servers and no user accounts. We do not collect, receive, or process any personal information, usage data, crash reports, device identifiers, or location data. The app's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

2. Location

TerraGrid uses your device's location to show your position, grid coordinates (MGRS, UTM, USNG, national grids and others), heading, altitude, and distances to waypoints. Location is processed on your device. It is used while the app is open, and in the background only while you are recording a track, navigating to a waypoint or along a route (for arrival alerts, voice guidance and the Live Activity), or broadcasting your position to your team. Each of these is started and stopped by you. Location is never sent to us or to any third party; the only time it leaves your device is team broadcasting (section 5).

3. Motion and altitude

With your permission (Motion & Fitness), TerraGrid reads the barometer to measure altitude and climb more precisely than GPS. Pressure and altitude readings are processed on your device and stored only as part of the tracks you record. You can turn barometric altitude off in Settings.

4. iCloud sync

Waypoints, folders, routes, tracks, shapes and reports are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is on, they are also stored in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit service, so they appear on your other devices. Only you can access that data; we cannot. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Team sharing

Team sharing is optional and uses CloudKit sharing in the iCloud account of the person who hosts the team. Only people the host invites can join, and we have no access to any team data.

  • Position broadcasting: while you broadcast, your call sign, coordinates and the time of the fix are written to the team about every 30 seconds. When you stop broadcasting, your position record is deleted. Team members see positions from the last six hours.
  • Shared waypoints and routes: when you choose Share with Team, the waypoint's or route's name, coordinates, symbol, colour, note and point names are shared, together with your call sign and a random identifier the app creates so that you can later remove what you shared. Shared items stay with the team until you remove them, you leave the team, or the host ends the team.
  • To show changes immediately, the app registers for Apple push notifications for the team's data. These are silent notifications sent by Apple's CloudKit service; they carry no content and are not used for anything else.

6. Camera and photos

The camera is used for the augmented-reality waypoint finder, scanning coordinate QR codes, and grid-stamped field photos. Photos you take are saved to your own photo library, with coordinates embedded in the image metadata (EXIF) when a position is available. Camera frames and photos you choose for coordinate text recognition are processed on-device and are never uploaded.

7. Map tiles

Map imagery is provided by Apple Maps and, when you download an offline map region, by the OpenTopoMap tile servers. When map tiles are requested, those providers receive standard network information such as your IP address and the map area requested, under their own privacy policies. Offline map downloads identify the app (as their usage policy requires) but carry no user or device identifier. Maps and elevation files you import yourself stay on your device.

8. Notifications

Arrival alerts, the track-recording reminder and twilight (BMNT/EENT) alerts are local notifications generated on your device. Nothing is pushed to you from a server, apart from the silent team notifications described in section 5.

9. Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Apple Watch

Siri requests such as "What's my grid?" are handled by Apple on-device or through Apple's services under Apple's privacy policy. Widgets and Live Activities read the same on-device data as the main app. The Apple Watch app exchanges waypoints and marked positions with your iPhone directly over Apple's WatchConnectivity; nothing is sent to us.

10. On-device AI assistant

On supported devices, the optional field assistant runs entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence foundation models. Your prompts and location are not sent to us or to any cloud service.

11. Exports and backups

GPX, KML, CSV, PDF and backup files you export are created on-device and shared only where you choose to send them.

12. Children

TerraGrid is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
